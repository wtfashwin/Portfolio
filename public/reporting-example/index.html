Definitions and formulas

  • Total spend (USD): sum(spend)
  • Impressions: sum(impressions)
  • Clicks: sum(clicks)
  • Source-reported leads: sum(source-reported leads)
  • Click-through rate (%): clicks / impressions * 100
  • Cost per reported lead (USD): spend / reported leads

Leads are additive source-reported counts, not unique customers or cross-platform attribution. No inferred or missing values are filled. Ratios and changes are rounded to two decimal places using half-up rounding; JSON preserves exact spend totals as decimal strings.
